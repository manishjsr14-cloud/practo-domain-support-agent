Question: What is the cancellation and rescheduling window?
top1=0.604042053; threshold=0.304713219; fallback=False

Question: How long do routine and specialized lab test reports take?
top1=0.687533259; threshold=0.304713219; fallback=False
Answer: Routine blood-test reports are usually available within 1-2 working days after the laboratory accepts the sample. Specialized tests usually take 3-5 working days, and the laboratory gives a test-specific estimate when the sample is accepted. Weekends, holidays, and a request for another sample can extend that estimate. Reception can check whether a report is ready, while a clinician must explain what the results mean.

Question: When does a follow-up visit qualify for a discount?
top1=0.693366587; threshold=0.304713219; fallback=False
Answer: A follow-up consultation within 7 calendar days of a completed visit qualifies for a 20 percent consultation-fee discount when it is with the same specialty for the same issue. Reception checks the original appointment reference and confirms eligibility before booking the follow-up. A record marked follow_up_required indicates that follow-up was requested but does not by itself prove discount eligibility. Lab tests, procedures, and travel charges are excluded from the discount.
