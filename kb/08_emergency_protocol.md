# Emergency Protocol and Support Boundaries (fictional clinic)

The clinic's appointment-support service does not provide emergency care or schedule it through routine chat. If you report a medical emergency, support must direct you to local emergency services or the nearest emergency department without asking you to wait for a routine appointment or chatbot review. Support must not diagnose, recommend medication, or use the administrative appointment-escalation score to judge medical urgency. Appointment status, payment confirmation, and insurance paperwork are not requirements for seeking emergency care. The support service can give emergency-service directions and recommend human help, but it does not dispatch ambulances, perform clinical triage, or create emergency admissions.
