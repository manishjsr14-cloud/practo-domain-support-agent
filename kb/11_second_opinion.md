# Second-Opinion Request Policy (fictional clinic)

You can request a second-opinion appointment with a different clinician by stating the specialty and providing any available documents from your previous consultation. Staff acknowledge a complete request within three business days and offer an appointment subject to clinician and slot availability. A second opinion is a separate consultation charged at the receiving specialty's standard fee and does not qualify for the same-clinician follow-up discount. Previous consultation documents are shared with the reviewing clinician only through the clinic's approved process and with your consent. Support can explain the arrangements, but it does not compare diagnoses, decide which clinician is correct, or promise a different clinical conclusion.
