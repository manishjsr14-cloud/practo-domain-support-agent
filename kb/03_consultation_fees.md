# Consultation fees by specialty - fictional clinic policy

General Medicine consultations cost INR 300-600, and Pediatrics consultations cost INR 400-800. Dermatology consultations cost INR 500-1000, and Orthopedics consultations cost INR 600-1200. Cardiology consultations cost INR 800-1500. The exact consultation fee within the specialty range is shown before the appointment is confirmed. Lab tests, procedures, and home-visit travel charges are quoted separately and are not included in the consultation fee.
