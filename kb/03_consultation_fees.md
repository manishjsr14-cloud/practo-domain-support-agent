# Consultation Fees by Specialty (fictional clinic)

Standard consultation fees for new bookings are INR 500 for General Medicine, INR 1200 for Cardiology, INR 800 for Dermatology, INR 600 for Pediatrics, and INR 1000 for Orthopedics. Each fee covers one consultation with the booked clinician and excludes medicines, laboratory tests, procedures, and home-visit travel charges. For an existing appointment, the consultation fee recorded in that booking is authoritative and may differ from the standard fee because of a documented adjustment. An eligible follow-up receives the discount described in the follow-up-discount policy, calculated from the specialty's standard consultation fee. Clinic staff must quote separate charges before confirmation; support must use the listed prices and leave recorded fees unchanged.
