# Follow-Up-Visit Discount Policy (fictional clinic)

A follow-up visit receives a 20 percent consultation-fee discount when it takes place within seven calendar days after a completed consultation with the same clinician, in the same specialty, and for the same concern. An eligible follow-up costs 80 percent of that specialty's standard new-booking fee; medicines, tests, procedures, and any home-visit travel charge are excluded from the discount. Only one discounted follow-up is allowed for each original completed consultation, and it cannot be combined with another consultation-fee offer. A visit after seven calendar days, with another clinician, or for a different concern costs the standard consultation fee unless staff record a separate adjustment. A recommendation for follow-up alone does not establish eligibility; staff must check the consultation dates, clinician, specialty, and concern.
