# Follow-up-visit discounts - fictional clinic policy

A follow-up consultation within 7 calendar days of a completed visit qualifies for a 20 percent consultation-fee discount when it is with the same specialty for the same issue. Reception checks the original appointment reference and confirms eligibility before booking the follow-up. A record marked follow_up_required indicates that follow-up was requested but does not by itself prove discount eligibility. Lab tests, procedures, and travel charges are excluded from the discount.
