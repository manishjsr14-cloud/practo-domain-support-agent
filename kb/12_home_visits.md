# Home-Visit Eligibility and Charges Policy (fictional clinic)

Non-emergency home visits are available only for General Medicine patients aged 18 years or older with an address within five kilometres of the clinic. Visit slots run from 09:00 to 17:00 local clinic time, and staff must confirm clinician availability and address eligibility. A standard home visit costs INR 800 in total: INR 500 for the General Medicine consultation plus a separate INR 300 travel surcharge, with any eligible follow-up discount applied only to the consultation fee. The travel surcharge is refunded only when a cancellation qualifies under the cancellation policy and occurs before the clinician is dispatched; consultation-fee refunds follow that policy. Clinic staff arrange address checks, clinician approval, and booking; support can explain the rules but cannot verify an address, approve suitability, reserve a clinician, or book a visit.
