# Patient-data privacy - fictional clinic policy

Patient records may be accessed only by authorized clinic staff for an approved care or administrative purpose. Use an appointment record ID for support queries and send sensitive documents only through the secure clinic portal. Requests to correct or delete records go to the privacy desk, which verifies the requester's authority before acting. This educational prototype accepts fabricated examples only, so do not submit real patient names, contact numbers, insurance identifiers, or medical records.
