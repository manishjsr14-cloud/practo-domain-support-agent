# Patient Privacy Policy (fictional clinic)

All patient records and contact details used in this fictional demonstration must be fabricated; do not enter real patient information or publish it in the project repository. Standalone 10-digit contact numbers, optionally prefixed by +91, must be masked before text enters model prompts, stored conversation history, or persisted request logs. Names, diagnoses or conditions, and insurance identifiers are not guaranteed to be masked and must also remain fictional. Conversation history stays in process memory until the session is reset or the process exits, and sanitized request logs may be kept for no more than seven days for troubleshooting. A session ID identifies a conversation rather than proving patient identity, so real patient appointment lookup requires a separate authorization system.
