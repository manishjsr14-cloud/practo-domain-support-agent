# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy (fictional clinic)

You can cancel or reschedule without a change fee if clinic staff receive your request at least 24 hours before the appointment starts. A cancellation within this window receives a full refund of the consultation fee recorded for the appointment; a later patient cancellation or a No-Show receives no consultation-fee refund. Rescheduling depends on an available slot, keeps your original paid consultation fee, and is confirmed only when staff mark the appointment Rescheduled. If the clinic cancels, you receive a full consultation-fee refund regardless of notice, and approved refunds return to the original payment method within seven business days. The support service explains these rules, but clinic staff must make booking changes and approve refunds.
