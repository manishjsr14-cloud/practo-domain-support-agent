# Lab-test turnaround times - fictional clinic policy

Routine blood-test reports are usually available within 1-2 working days after the laboratory accepts the sample. Specialized tests usually take 3-5 working days, and the laboratory gives a test-specific estimate when the sample is accepted. Weekends, holidays, and a request for another sample can extend that estimate. Reception can check whether a report is ready, while a clinician must explain what the results mean.
