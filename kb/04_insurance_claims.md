# Insurance-Claim Support Policy (fictional clinic)

Patients pay the clinic directly and can request documents for insurance reimbursement; cashless treatment and direct insurer billing are not offered. To request insurance documents, provide your appointment record ID, payment receipt, and any document checklist from your insurer. After staff verify that the appointment is Completed and paid, they provide an itemized consultation receipt and available clinician-approved visit documents within three business days. Your insurer decides coverage, claim deadlines, approval, and reimbursement amounts, so payment of a claim is not guaranteed. The support service can explain how to request documents; submitting claims, accessing insurer accounts, and deciding insurance eligibility are outside its role.
